Delete your Tessaya account and data

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Tessaya has no central store of your conversations. What you create lives on the Tessaya server you run, and the app keeps only what it needs on your phone.

Steps

  1. Data on your server. Delete threads, voice recordings and files in Tessaya on your own server, or remove its data folder. Deletion there is immediate and complete.
  2. This phone. In the app, open Settings and remove the paired server, or uninstall the app. This deletes the pairing credential, push token and cached data on the phone.
  3. Connection sign-in. If you signed in to the optional connection service, open an issue at github.com/RabHanz/tessaya-legal/issues titled "Delete my account" (do not post personal details publicly; we will reply with a private way to confirm). Your email address and account identifier are deleted within 30 days. Nothing is kept after that, apart from records the law requires us to keep.

See also the privacy policy.
