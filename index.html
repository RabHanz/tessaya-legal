Tessaya Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Tessaya is a phone app for talking to and directing AI agents that run on a Tessaya server you connect it to. This policy covers the Tessaya app for Android and iOS.

Where your data goes

The app talks to the Tessaya server you pair it with, either directly on your network or over an encrypted tunnel. That server runs on machines you control. We do not run a central service that receives your conversations, audio or files.

What the app handles

  • Account and pairing details. A pairing credential for your server, kept in the phone's secure storage. If you sign in to an optional connection service, your email address and account identifier are used to sign you in.
  • Voice audio. When you use voice input, the microphone records only while you are speaking to the app. The audio is sent to your own server for transcription and processing.
  • Messages and files. What you type, and photos or files you choose to attach, are sent to your server as part of the conversation and stored there.
  • Camera. Used only to scan a pairing QR code. Images are not stored or sent.
  • Device data. A device identifier and a push notification token, so your server can recognise this phone and send it notifications; basic device details (model, operating system version) for compatibility.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with data brokers.
  • No third-party tracking or analytics SDKs.

Third parties

Push notifications are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) or Apple Push Notification service (iOS). App updates are downloaded from Expo's update service. Agent providers you choose to use on your server (for example a large language model provider) receive what you send to them under their own terms.

Keeping and deleting data

Your conversations, audio and files live on your own server, and you can delete them there at any time. Removing the app deletes everything it stored on the phone, including the pairing credential.

Children

Tessaya is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Contact
